Vyuhbhed — Privacy Policy

Effective 7 September 2026 · applies to the Vyuhbhed Android, iOS and web apps (package in.vickysolutions.vyuhbhed)

The short version

Vyuhbhed runs on your device by default. If you paste your own Gemini API key in Settings, the text you send to the coach is sent to Google under Google's terms; the key and everything else stay on the phone. It has no account system, no analytics, no advertising SDK and no server of its own. Nothing you type into Vyuhbhed is transmitted anywhere.

What the app stores, and where

Your journal, your origin story, your conversations with the counsellor and your settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for this app, so this data does not leave the device even through system backups. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Network access

The app connects to the internet for exactly one purpose: downloading the language-model file the counsellor runs on, once, from a public GitHub Releases page. That download carries no personal data and no identifier beyond what any web download carries (your IP address, seen by GitHub under GitHub's privacy statement). After the model is downloaded, the counsellor works fully offline.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect, receive, store or sell any personal data. We cannot see what you write. There is nothing for us to share with third parties.

Permissions

Biometric / device-lock permission is used only to lock the app if you turn that on; authentication happens through the operating system and the app never sees your biometric data. Notifications, if enabled, are scheduled locally on the device.

Safety content

Vyuhbhed includes an on-device safety check that may show helpline numbers when a conversation suggests someone is at risk. That check runs on your phone; nothing is reported to us or to anyone else.

Not medical or professional advice

Vyuhbhed is a self-help tool. It is not a licensed therapist, counsellor or medical service and does not replace one. If you are in danger, contact local emergency services or the helplines listed in the app.

Children

Vyuhbhed is intended for adults (18+) and is rated accordingly on the stores.

Changes and contact

If a future version starts sending any data off the device (for example an optional couple-sync feature), this policy will be updated first and the app will ask for your consent in-app before that feature is used. Questions: pallavsharmaofficial@gmail.com.
